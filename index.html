Getting to Houston

Choose the route that works for you.

I'm not telling anybody how to travel. I'm just making sure you know what the ground trip looks like once you get here.

IAH

George Bush Intercontinental

~28 miles · ~40–45 min typical drive
Generally the more convenient airport for getting to Cypress.

HOU

William P. Hobby

~40 miles · ~60–65 min typical drive
Absolutely an option, especially if the fare or airline works better. Just plan for the longer drive.

🚗

Driving from Atlanta?

Plan roughly 13–14+ hours with normal fuel, food, bathroom and family stops—plus holiday traffic.

A little Houston wisdom: Travel times can change quickly with traffic. Give yourself some breathing room, especially around the holiday. We'll be here when you arrive.
